Season Ticket Tracker

Privacy

Last updated 6 October 2026

Who this is for

Ticket Tracker is a personal app, built and used by one person for their own commute. It isn't offered to the public, and nobody else's data is collected.

What Google access is used for

  • Google Drive — to save backup copies of the app's own data into a "Ticket Tracker Backups" folder, and, if you turn on sharing with Personal Finance, a small summary file of your monthly ticket and parking estimates and your season ticket purchases in the same folder. The access granted only covers files this app created itself; it can't see anything else in your Drive.
  • Google Calendar — to keep a single "Buy train ticket" reminder up to date. It doesn't read your other events.

Where data lives

  • On your device — the app's records (travel days, tickets, delay repay claims and settings) are stored in your browser on your phone.
  • In your own Google Drive — backups, if you turn them on, and the estimates summary (transport-estimates.json), if you turn on sharing with Personal Finance. The summary holds only the pay-period dates, ticket and parking-day counts and their costs, plus each season ticket's dates, purchase date and price — no travel history, claims or account details. It's read by your own Personal Finance app, which uses the same Google access.
  • In your own Cloudflare Worker — live train lookups, the settings your watch needs, and, if you connect automatic sign-in, the Google sign-in token that keeps backups running without a tap.

Nothing is sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising.

Removing access

You can remove Ticket Tracker's access to your Google account at any time from Google Account → Security → Third-party access, or with Disconnect automatic sign-in on the app's Backup & restore screen. Deleting the app's data from your browser removes everything stored on the device.
